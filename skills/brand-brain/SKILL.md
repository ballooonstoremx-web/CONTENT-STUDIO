# Brand Brain

1. Read `brand/profile.json` (facts) and `brand/BRAND-BRAIN.md` (rules). Do not
   restate them elsewhere; link to them.
2. When reviewing a draft, check in this order and report each as pass/fail
   with the exact line:
   - invented product, model, packaging, price, stock or delivery claim;
   - product shown without a real photo (or a generated stand-in);
   - unconfirmed technical claim without a source;
   - placeholder `[confirmar con catálogo real]` still present in a final;
   - off-palette colors, text in Reel/Story unsafe zones, missing CTA;
   - voice: Mexican Spanish, "tú", B2B audience.
3. For every missing asset, output a request: what file, framing, background,
   orientation and who can provide it.
4. Never approve on the owner's behalf; recommend a status only.
