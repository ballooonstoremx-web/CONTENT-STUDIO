# Product marketing context (pointer)

Third-party marketing skills (copywriting, cro, emails, seo-audit, etc.) look for this file.
Balloon Store keeps a single source of brand truth, so this file holds no facts:

- Rules, voice and judgment: `brand/BRAND-BRAIN.md`
- Facts (audience, product lines, colors, site, channels): `brand/profile.json`
- Real products: `catalog/catalog.json` (read-only Shopify export, no prices or stock)

Read those three files instead of asking the owner again. Do not copy their content here.

If the `product-marketing` skill offers to fill or update this file, update `brand/BRAND-BRAIN.md` or `brand/profile.json` instead (owner approval required) and keep this file as a pointer.
