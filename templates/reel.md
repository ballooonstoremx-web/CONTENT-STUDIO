# Reel production plan

This is a project workflow; the carousel skill explicitly excludes video.

1. Confirm goal, audience, approved offer, product references and video/voice/music
   rights. Target 1080×1920, 9:16, MP4 H.264 yuv420p. Project delivery duration:
   1–180 seconds, with 15–30 seconds suggested; check current platform limits.
2. Write the hook and spoken script in COPY.md before generating footage.
3. Storyboard in a table: timecode | shot | movement | voiceover | on-screen copy
   | reference / rights | source / generation job. Suggested pacing: 0–3s hook,
   3–12s demonstration, 12–22s value, 22–30s confirmed CTA.
4. Discover a real connected video tool and verify supported models and costs.
   No Higgsfield video tools were connected during setup. If unavailable, use
   rights-cleared existing footage and manual editing in CapCut or another
   approved editor. Do not claim storyboard text is a rendered video.
5. Keep raw clips in raw/. Check frame-to-frame balloon shape, strings, hands,
   logos, scene continuity, color and motion. Avoid synthetic customer claims.
6. Edit clips, licensed audio and approved overlays in an available editor. Keep
   important copy away from platform UI; preview safe areas on target devices.
   Write subtitles in review/captions.srt; verify sync and readability.
7. Export exactly one MP4 into finals/. Run final check (requires ffprobe).
   Watch the complete video with sound and muted, review accessibility and facts,
   record provenance and human approval, then package. No posting or deployment.

FFmpeg/ffprobe are local media utilities, not generative video or editing-service
connections. Canva and CapCut remain manual tools until a real integration exists.
