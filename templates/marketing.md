# Marketing copy production plan

1. Confirm audience, goal, format, language, exact product facts, offer dates,
   verified website destination and CTA. Do not infer shipping area or inventory.
2. Write three angles in COPY.md, then pick one: educational value, event planning,
   or approved product spotlight. Keep the reader's benefit concrete.
3. Draft channel-specific headline, body, CTA and optional hashtags in CAPTIONS.md.
   Suggested formats: Instagram caption, email subject/body, product description,
   campaign concept. Do not send emails, create ads or schedule posts.
4. Verify every factual claim and reference; remove invented discounts,
   testimonials and scarcity. Use approved brand voice and accessible language.
5. Put reviewed deliverables in finals/ as Markdown or text. Record sources and
   usage rights. Complete review/approval.json, then export for owner review.

Useful tentative angles: balloon care checklist, choosing an event palette, or
planning a decoration corner. These are ideas, not verified product claims.
