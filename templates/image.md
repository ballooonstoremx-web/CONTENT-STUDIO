# Realistic AI marketing image production plan

This is a project workflow; the attached carousel skill does not cover it.

1. Confirm subject, product truth, intended placement, exact size, approved
   reference photos, right to use the location/people, and CTA outside the image.
2. Write one prompt in COPY.md (or image-prompt.md): subject and balloon material,
   arrangement, real scale, scene, camera angle/lens, lighting, background,
   Balloon Store palette as art direction, and space reserved for later text.
3. Negative constraints: no extra balloon strings, impossible knots, warped logos,
   distorted hands, fake testimonials, invented products, or unrelated branding.
4. Discover connected image tools. Higgsfield is preferred only when available
   and its current model supports the need. An OpenAI image tool may be offered
   separately, but requires an explicit provider choice and no hidden substitution.
   Verify cost and obtain spending approval before a paid job. Do not upload
   private references without permission.
5. Record model, prompt, references, date, job ID and edits in PROVENANCE.md.
   Keep original output in raw/. Check realism at 100%, references, materials,
   colors and honest representation. Clearly label mockups as AI concepts.
6. Place one reviewed PNG/JPEG at 1080×1350 in finals/. Fit-and-pad or compose to
   that size; do not crop away the product. Overlay exact logo/text only from
   approved assets in an available design editor. Write alt text and caption.
7. Fill human review checks and export. No automatic publishing.

Prompt starter: "Photorealistic [confirmed arrangement] using [material and sizes]
in [scene], [lighting], [camera/viewpoint], natural latex reflections, physically
plausible strings and knots, blue #54C1FA and pink #EA4566 accents, [composition],
clean space for approved copy, no text, no watermark, no fabricated logo."
